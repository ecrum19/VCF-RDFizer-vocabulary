# Acknowledgements

The VCF-RDFizer vocabulary is developed at [KNoWS](https://knows.idlab.ugent.be/),
IDLab, Ghent University - imec, as part of the doctoral research of
Elias Crum (<elias.crum@ugent.be>).

Please cite the vocabulary as described in [`CITATION.cff`](CITATION.cff).

## Acknowledgement sentence for papers

When this vocabulary is used in a publication, the following template can be
adapted for the Acknowledgements section:

> This work was carried out at KNoWS, IDLab, Ghent University - imec, as part
> of the doctoral research of Elias Crum. The vocabulary builds on the VCF 4.5
> specification and aligns to SB/gvar, HERO, FALDO, GENO, SIO, and GA4GH VRS;
> the authors thank their maintainers, and those of the open-source tools used
> to publish and validate it - Ontology Companion Generator and pySHACL.

If the work is co-funded by a specific project or grant, add that project name
and grant number to the sentence above.

## Related work

The companion conversion tool is
[VCF-RDFizer](https://github.com/ecrum19/VCF-RDFizer), which produces graphs in
both representation profiles described here and validates them against these
shapes.
